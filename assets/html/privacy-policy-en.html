Privacy Policy

Smart Rent is committed to protecting your personal data and wishes to inform you that when using the Smart Rent website or app, it collects, uses, processes, and discloses your personal information confidentially and in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law issued by the Council of Ministers Decision No. (98) dated 07/02/1443H. Therefore, Smart Rent has created this privacy policy to demonstrate its commitment to protecting the privacy of all users on its platform or app.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. By using the Smart Rent website or app, all your information is subject to this policy.

Scope of the Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers personal information maintained in the electronic system, including:

  • Personal information you provide.
  • Personal information obtained from external sources.
  • Information collected automatically when using the website or app.

Personal Information Collected by Smart Rent

Personal information refers to data that users provide when using Smart Rent services (website or app) to allow Smart Rent Communications and IT Company to identify users on its platform. These include:

  • Account Data: When registering, you will be asked for personal information, e.g., name, address, email.
  • Identity & Verification Data (KYC): Identity verification is required by regulations to confirm driving eligibility, including: National ID/Iqama, driving license photo, and profile picture, captured via camera or uploaded from your photo library with your explicit permission.
  • Correspondence: When communicating with customer service via phone, email, or platform channels, we collect contact information and any other details you provide to serve you better.

Information Collected Automatically by the Website or App

Non-personal information automatically sent by your browser or app to our servers, including:

  • Usage Data: Information about your interactions with our services, such as pages viewed, bookings made, and navigation paths.
  • Location Data (When In Use Only): We access your approximate or precise location solely while actively using the app to show the nearest available vehicles and rental branches, verify delivery coverage, and calculate delivery fees. We do NOT track location in the background.
  • Device Data: Information about your device and operating system for compatibility and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Used to understand how services are utilized on the website or app.

Personal Information from External Sources

This includes information collected when using third-party services, such as logging into Smart Rent via Google, Facebook, or Apple. These third parties may authorize us to access your registration information and profile data. You agree that Smart Rent may collect and use this information according to this privacy policy.

Purpose of Using Personal Information

  • Account Creation: To create and manage your account, enabling use of services, bookings, transactions, offers, and responding to inquiries.
  • Bookings: To complete and manage bookings and send relevant details to service providers.
  • Customer Service: To respond to inquiries and provide assistance.
  • Marketing Activities: To analyze and promote Smart Rent services through marketing campaigns.
  • Communication: To verify your account and send operational or promotional messages.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze and enhance products and services.
  • Legal Purposes: To handle legal disputes, enforce terms, or comply with legal requests.

Third Parties with Whom We Share Personal Data

  • Car Rental Companies: Share booking details with the service providers to complete reservations and hand over the car.
  • Service Providers & Payment Gateways: Payments are processed securely and encrypted via licensed gateways (Moyasar, Apple Pay). Sensitive card details are not stored on our servers.
  • Legal Requests: Disclose information to courts, government authorities, or related parties as required by law.

Customer Ratings

We may request service ratings to improve services. These ratings represent your opinion and may be modified or removed at Smart Rent’s discretion.

Information Security

We implement standard encryption (SSL/TLS) and organizational safeguards to protect your information, but cannot guarantee complete online security.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Smart Rent reserves the right to update this privacy policy and will notify users of any changes through posting the updated policy.

Updating Your Personal Data

You can review, correct, and update your personal information via your account settings or by contacting us. Identity verification may be required.

Consent

By reading this privacy policy, you consent to the collection and use of personal information as described. If you do not agree, please do not provide personal information.

Right to Delete Account and Personal Data (Account Deletion)

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL):

  • In-App Deletion: You have the right to delete your account at any time directly inside the app by navigating to: (Side Menu -> Delete Account) and confirming the request.
  • Immediate Data Removal: Account deletion immediately removes your name, profile photo, identity documents, and unlinks your phone number and email, allowing you to re-register with your phone number as a fresh new user anytime.
  • Regulatory Record Keeping: Financial invoice records and completed transaction logs are retained strictly in anonymized format for statutory periods required by Saudi tax and accounting regulations.
